A single deadpan weather forecast for Grumbleville, a city that does not exist. Delivered in the flat, confident tone of a TV meteorologist who has seen it all: a high of 14 degrees, and light hail made of unopened letters falling until noon. The joke lands in the second half — residents are told to keep their postboxes closed and their expectations low — which is exactly the kind of practical advice a real forecaster would give if the sky were raining mail.

## The entry

> In Grumbleville, expect a high of 14 degrees and light hail of unopened letters until noon; residents should keep postboxes closed and expectations low.

## How it works

Run `node main.ts` (Node 22 or later). It writes the single forecast line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after a length gate that throws unless the line is 1–160 characters. No network, no randomness — the output is fully deterministic.
